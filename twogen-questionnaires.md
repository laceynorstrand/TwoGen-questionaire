# TwoGen Market Research Questionnaires

## Introduction

TwoGen is a startup business concept being developed as part of graduate school work at Montana State University. We hope to launch the service within the next year. This questionnaire will help us understand people’s interests and concerns so we can design a practical framework for the TwoGen model. Participation is voluntary, every question is optional, and no personal data will be stored.

---
